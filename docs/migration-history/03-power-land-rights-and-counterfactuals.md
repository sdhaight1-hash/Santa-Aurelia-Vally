# Political Succession, Rights, Displacement and Alternate-History Stress Tests

## Foundational principle: arrival is not legitimate title
Santa Aurelia's 1608 founding **cannot** erase existing Indigenous residents or law. Trade and marriages create relationships, but a founder's generosity or romance does not automatically grant Spanish ownership of a region. Territorial boundaries, fishery access, sacred sites, arbitration, tribute/tax conflicts, disease, coercion, religious conversion, class and gender inequalities, and Indigenous strategic choices need a coherent history. A positive local tradition of negotiated relations can coexist with serious wrongs and disputed accounts.

Sáweli/Inés is an author and Indigenous political actor with her own perspective; her descendants do not constitute the entire Indigenous population, and descent alone is not a substitute for tribal citizenship or governmental recognition.

## The continental counterfactual's biggest structural problem
In real history, Astor's post was founded in **1811** and went through British trade-company succession. In our setting a thriving **1608-established Spanish-linked port** occupies the same site. This changes regional geopolitics, shipping, diplomacy, commerce, land claims, the balance between Indigenous polities and imperial actors, and the terms under which any later American state authority arrives.

### Necessary questions before filling exact dates
1. Was the port initially under a viceregal Spanish charter, private commercial concession, or other licensing arrangement? **1608 charter text U**.
2. How did it sustain sea communication and military security through the seventeenth century? Number of vessels, return routes, provisions, navigational feasibility and legal monopolies **U**.
3. What changed after Mexican independence (1821), and what did local families regard as legitimate? **Not determined**.
4. How did British maritime fur companies respond to an existing city in 1811–1846? **Must diverge from real Astoria**.
5. How and when did Santa Aurelia and its inland surroundings enter a United States framework? Were treaties, purchase, negotiated terms or local transitional institutions involved? **Not determined**.
6. How were earlier Indigenous, Spanish, mixed-family and merchant land rights adjudicated, lost, preserved or disputed? **U**.
7. Which in-setting event gives the reservation its present constitutional, treaty and jurisdictional status? **U**, to be developed with careful distinction from real modern tribal nations.

**Do not** choose an arbitrary patriotic transition date and then treat all later real U.S. laws as operating retroactively. A localized divergence is plausible to worldbuild; a centuries-long European colony at the Columbia mouth with zero ripple effects elsewhere is not.

## Why the migrations can nevertheless coexist
A major port can continue attracting multinational sailors and traders through shifting empires. Merchant Venetians can invest where ocean trade and elite clients exist. Connecticut and Virginia families can choose mining/mineral resources in an inland valley when legal security and transport access improve. European immigrants can cluster in outer timber/mining districts through chain migration, while individual members move onward and marry. Chinese and Finnish workers can find industrial coastal work if a canning economy develops, but their actual numbers cannot be borrowed from the real 1880 census as though this were the identical Astoria.

## Law and discrimination belong in the model
In the **real** Pacific Northwest, Chinese laborers were vital to salmon canning and faced exclusion, exploitation and restricted opportunities. Oregon and U.S. policies also constrained Asian migration and landholding; Japanese Americans faced wartime forced removal and incarceration.

**Working fictional editorial requirement:** Once the territorial incorporation and federal-law timeline is defined, determine how these laws operated locally, who resisted, who profited, and how immigrant households and sovereign Indigenous communities experienced them. Do not assume Santa Aurelia's admirable romance makes racism, class hierarchies or forced displacement impossible. Also do not copy real historical persecutions onto named fictional families as settled details without deciding their histories.

Local migrant institutions must have people with agency: organizers, mothers, merchants, mutual-aid leaders, religious associations, workers, artisans, educators and civic advocates—not merely roles of victimhood or aesthetic decoration.

## Settlement ecology and land-use constraints
- The roughly **1,800 sq mi cultural region** combines multiple watersheds; it is **not a single vast tillable valley**.
- The 2026 working population of **~60,000** does not authorize historic population estimates or exact ethnic percentages.
- High Coast Range slopes, Cascadia hazard zones, wet floodplains and marine exposure restrict urban and rural development.
- The ancient Witches' Wood core is not a free logging concession or empty migrant homestead zone. Its protection by reputation, failed logging and active tribal/Fernhollow advocacy coexist with **varied land title and no overarching formal designation yet**.
- Cattle farms, 4-H and rodeo exist principally on suitable **South Hills, valley-floor and outer rural** terrain.
- The native Blacksilver Willow has been propagated across the region from cuttings for centuries; woodworking stock predominantly comes from **cultivated trees outside the ancient forest**, not an industrial harvest of the protected wild giants.
- Public/sensitive maps may show only generalized reservation or monument reference centers, not surveyed boundaries or invitations to trespass.

## Migration-accounting principles
**Four kinds of movement must be tracked separately:**
- **Arrivals** from outside the region.
- **Internal resettlement** between coast, river valleys, mining town, Blackwood margins and farms.
- **Return/seasonal migration** by sailors, cannery laborers, pastoral workers, students and migrants maintaining more than one home.
- **Departures and displacement** caused by disasters, industry collapse, laws, families and personal choices.

A chronological roster without these will exaggerate growth. Beware **founder effect**: 67 ship passengers are a *departure muster*, not 67 additional net permanent inhabitants; not all later descendants stay.
