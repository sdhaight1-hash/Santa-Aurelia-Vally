# Continuity register — decisions, revisions, and open development

This register protects **latest user-stated choices** from drifting back into discarded assistant ideas.

## Explicit later revisions that supersede earlier drafts
**Regional title:** replace Willowwick-centric setting framing with **Valle de Santa Aurelia** as the region; keep **Willowwick Grove** as inland city, and **Santa Aurelia** as coastal city.

**European founding:** supersede earlier proposed **1853**, **1810**, and **1772** colonial start dates. **1608** is the first European colonial permanent settlement date. Indigenous residence long predates it.

**Founding place:** Santa Aurelia is specifically **at real-world Astoria**; not an invented remote unnamed coastal harbor. This necessarily changes John Jacob Astor/Fort Astoria's real 1811 narrative.

**Earthquake:** earlier drafts made the 1700 Cascadia disaster effectively relocate/recreate the port inland. Revised canon: **Santa Aurelia rebuilds and remains substantial**, while an inland settlement corridor also expands.

**Population:** Santa Aurelia is nearly Willowwick Grove's equal, **~19,000 vs ~21,000** working figure, greater region ~60,000; not a minor village.

**Old-money origins:** eastern American Connecticut/Virginia migrations and Ashcroft/Blackthorn-related fortunes are **primarily Willowwick**. Santa Aurelia's elite comes mainly from older Spanish-Indigenous maritime families and **later Venetian investment**.

**Architecture:** Santa Aurelia distinctive Spanish–Indigenous **and later Venetian** regional style; Willowwick primarily Victorian/Gothic/garden-city. Do not transfer architectural histories indiscriminately.

**Alonso and Inés:** the union is **mutual passionate love**, not a marriage primarily arranged for diplomacy. It also yields important political kinship consequences. Their descendants survive across social classes.

**Ball:** seventeenth-century formal assembly and ball-hall, progressively expanded; modern debutante forms and romantic festival dedication arrived later. Do not portray the ball as founded in Victorian era.

**Forest names:** **Fairy Stones** is preferred common name of stone circle in luminous Silverveil Grove; **Giants' Ring** remains myth-linked alternative; Witches' Wood contains both the stones and endemic giant Blacksilver Willows.

**Forest geography:** reservation **includes northwest portions of Witches' Wood**, not merely adjacent; **Fernhollow southwest** and dispersed prepper/homestead communities; the main archaeological site has not been definitively assigned to reservation or non-reservation land.

## Detailed items that still need canonization
**Family:** Don Alonso de Valcárcel y Austria; his proposed birth/death 1572–1641; invented additional son of Don John of Austria; Spanish-recorded wife Inés; wedding 1611; historical document fragments and dates. The user requested royalty offshoot, riches, authentic love; exact genealogical branch and personal names originated as **assistant proposals**.

**Indigenous identity:** nation/name, political geography, specific chief, spouse's original language/name, elders' names, mixed-family roles and laws, tribe's sovereignty path, gaming land, accurate archaeological research permissions. Do not assign fabricated Chinookan spellings.

**Stone sites:** precise layout and counts, chronological strata, monumental stone sources, whether ancestral genetics genuinely prove a precolonial Atlantic-related signal, folklore transmission and many documented dates of supposed encounters. Do not turn speculative Celtic/Druidic analogies into proof.

**Geology:** ancient fragment vs full regional craton; mining discovery and ore formation chronology; river and lake origins; seismic risks and tsunami contour.

**Architecture:** 1638 founding assembly hall year, precise phases of Palacio expansion, Santa Aurelia flagship tonnage/original structure and dry-berth plan, Riva name, gardens and preservation ownership.

**Community names:** Glenmorrow, Valea Argintie, Starý Brod; names proposed, not individually confirmed. Fernhollow, Thornhaven, Alderwood, Blackwood, Willowwick, Santa Aurelia are established design concepts.

**Ecology:** taxonomic validity of `Salix argentenebra`, actual pollen records, giant size/lifespan, introduced vs relict populations, fungally specialized decomposer species and documented ecological mechanism, conservation boundary.

**Folklore:** no canonical supernatural events. Names such as Lantern Children, Blackwood Howler, White Ones/White Giants and Mystery Stoneways have been discussed, but the actual chronology of specific incidents is largely a creative proposal. 1967 Blackwood Road case, invented archival quotations and any first-person accounts need explicit treatment as in-setting creative documents.

## Latest addition — the founding portraits
Four surviving **seventeenth-century original** portraits of Alonso, Inés, their wedding, and the eventual family of **eleven (parents plus nine children)** were produced by one **famous Spanish artist**, an old **friend of Alonso who traveled with the 1608 founding colony**. This creator relationship, survival and nine-child count are **explicit user canon**. **Mateo de Villacorta** and exact painting dates are assistant **working proposals**, not user-approved names. The nine children are canonical but as-yet unnamed; do not manufacture a definitive genealogy. See [colonial portrait catalog](13-colonial-portraits-and-artist.md).

## Later correction — ownership of paintings and founding records
**Established and superseding earlier suggested museum custodians:** the *main Valcárcel line*, not the state, a museum or dispersed collections, has held **all four original colonial portraits and the original founding fleet logbooks** throughout the centuries in its own **private estate library**. Temporary conservation/exhibition or copies do not change that ownership. Estate name/location, 1700 preservation episode, access rules and a complete custody chain still need work. Reference: [Valcárcel Estate Library](17-valcarcel-estate-library-and-archive.md).

## New established archive scope
The main-line Valcárcel Estate Library additionally holds original **love letters between Alonso and Sáweli/Inés**, journals by many of the **1608 founding settlers**, **Sáweli/Inés's own diaries**, and a wide range of colonial-era physical artifacts. Crucially, her accounts of **pre-1608 Ashalewan childhood and tribal life are retrospective writings composed after learning to read and write as an adult**, not contemporaneous childhood manuscripts. The exact literacy dates, specific excerpts, object inventory numbers and detailed provenance remain working reconstruction. See [collection catalogue](18-estate-library-collection-catalogue.md).
